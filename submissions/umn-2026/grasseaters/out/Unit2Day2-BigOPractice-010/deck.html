Asymptotic analysis (big O notation stuff)

T⁡(N) = 3⁢N3 + 2⁢N − 10

Lower bound choices:

  • f⁡(N) = N3
  • k=1
  • N0=10
T⁡(N) ≥ k⋅f⁡(N) ∀N>N0
3⁢N3 +2⁢N−10 ≥ 1⋅N3 ∀N>10
2⁢N3 +2⁢N−10 ≥ 0 ∀N>10

Note that all growing terms are positive. At N=10, 2⁢N3≈2000, which is definitely greater than −10, and so the inequality holds at N≥10.

T⁡(N) = Ω (N3)

Tightening trick

3⁢N3 +2⁢N−10 ≥…

Tightened by zeroing out a term.

3⁢N3 +0−10 ≥…

Tightened by upgrading a negative term.

3⁢N3 +0−10⁢N3 ≥…

Oops! This left us with a negative expression greater than or equal to a positive expression, marked with a sad face.

T⁡(N) = 10⁢N2 − 4⁢N + 6
10⁢N2 − 4⁢N2 +0 ≥ k⋅f⁡(N)
6⁢N2 ≥ k⋅f⁡(N) ∀N>N0

Floor of log base 2 of N

T⁡(N) = ⌊ log2 ⁡N ⌋

Floor means rounded down to the nearest integer.

Upper bound choices:

  • f⁡(N) = N⁢log⁡N
  • k=1
  • N0=10
T⁡(N) ≤ k⋅f⁡(N) ∀N>N0
⌊ log2 ⁡N ⌋ ≤ 1⋅N⋅ log2 ⁡N ∀N>10

Tighten by noting that floor only decreases, so removing it increases.

log2 ⁡N ≤ N⋅ log2 ⁡N ∀N>10
1 ≤ N ∀N>10

This last line is marked with a check.

Lower bound for the floor of log

Lower bound choices:

  • f⁡(N) = log⁡N
  • k=12
  • N0=10
T⁡(N) ≥ k⋅f⁡(N) ∀N>N0

Tightened:

⌊ log2 ⁡N ⌋ ≥ 12 ⁢ log2 ⁡N ∀N>10
( log2 ⁡N ) −1 ≥ 12 ⁢ log2 ⁡N ∀N>10
12 ⁢ log2 ⁡N ≥ 1 ∀N>10
log2 ⁡N ≥ 2 ∀N>10

Perimeter of a regular pentagon

T⁡(N) equals the perimeter of a regular pentagon with height 3⁢N.

Upper bound choices:

  • f⁡(N)=N
  • k=15
  • N0=10
T⁡(N) ≤ k⋅f⁡(N) ∀N>N0

Use a proxy. The word proxy is underlined on the slide.

T⁡(N) ≤ proxy ≤ k⋅f⁡(N) ∀N>N0

Let the proxy be a regular pentagon with side length 3⁢N.

T⁡(N) ≤ proxy
